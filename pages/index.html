A closer look at each step of the process.

01

Define your job & questions

Create a job posting with structured questions tailored to the role. Set competency rubrics so every candidate is measured against the same criteria.

Takes ~5 minutes
02

Share a link with candidates

Send a unique interview link via email, Slack, or embed it on your careers page. Candidates can complete the interview anytime, from any device.

No app download needed
03

Candidates complete a voice interview

An AI interviewer asks each question via natural voice. Candidates respond verbally — just like a real phone screen. The system transcribes everything automatically.

~15 min per candidate
04

AI evaluates & ranks every candidate

Multiple specialized AI agents score each transcript against your rubrics. You receive a detailed report with scores, evidence, and a ranked shortlist — ready for human review.
