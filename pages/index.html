02 — Routing Slip

What happens between "candidate clicks link" and "recruiter sees a score"

Evaluation runs asynchronously on the sealed transcript — never live — so the interview itself stays fast on a weak connection.

01 · Recruiter

Job & rubric set

Configure the role, question set, and scoring weights once — reuse across the drive.

02 · Candidate

Voice interview

A structured, conversational interview over a link — works on low bandwidth, any device.

03 · Transcript

Speech to sealed text

The interview is transcribed and sealed — the record every downstream score is checked against.

04 · Agent pipeline

Specialized evaluation

Skill, integrity, and scoring agents each read the sealed transcript and produce their piece of the report.

05 · Recruiter

Ranked, explainable list

A filterable shortlist where every score links back to the evidence that produced it.

04 — Ground Conditions

Designed for the drive as it actually happens

Not a global HR tool retrofitted for India — built against Tier 2/3 connectivity and real candidate behavior from day one.

Connectivity fallback ladder Video drops to audio, audio drops to async response, async drops to a phone call — the interview doesn't just fail on a bad connection.
Multilingual & code-mixed Candidates answer the way they actually speak — including switching languages mid-answer.
WhatsApp & SMS delivery Interview links reach candidates on the channel they actually check, not just email.
India-only data residency Encryption and access control designed around where the data is allowed to live.
Bias tested across slices Explainability checked against language and accent — not just overall accuracy.
